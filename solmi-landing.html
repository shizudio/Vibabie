Frequently asked questions.

How long does it take to make a music video?
The free five-second preview renders in about twenty seconds. A full-length video takes roughly three minutes once you have approved the storyboard — you are not waiting on a queue while you decide.
What audio can I bring?
Upload a file, choose something from your library, or paste a Suno, TikTok or YouTube link. If you do not have a track to hand, the dice will pick one for you so you can see what Solmi does before committing anything.
Do I need the rights to the song?
