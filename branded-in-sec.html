Branded in Sec

A tool that turns a brand's palette, type, and logo into ready-to-post social media assets in seconds — no design software, no drift from the guidelines. Built first as an internal tool for a client, now becoming its own product.

Next.js  ·  React  ·  OpenAI 2026

What it does

Feed it a brand kit — colours, typography, logo — and it generates a set of on-brand post templates: photo + logo, quote card, event announcement, plain text post, photo + caption. Everything renders at the brand's exact palette and type, so nothing needs a second pass in Figma before it goes out.

An AI Art Director sits on top: describe the post in a sentence and it proposes a template, layout, copy direction, colour, and logo placement to start from. Drag to reposition, scroll to resize, export at 1080×1080. Every upload and export is saved for reuse, so a content calendar builds itself as you go.

Why it exists

Built first as a private tool for a client's own social team, so posts stay on-brand without a designer in the loop for every asset. The template and brand-kit system underneath isn't client-specific — it's becoming its own product: brand it in seconds, for any brand, not just one.
