SHIZUDIO Coffee

Product design concept · 2026

A coffee machine concept that adapts grind, dose and water temperature to the room around it, while keeping you in the making. You choose the coffee, it handles the variables, and a little feedback after each cup makes the next one more yours.

One grinder, three ways to brew: espresso on the machine, pour-over and French press from freshly prepared grounds. The behaviour shown is proposed and has not been engineering-validated.
