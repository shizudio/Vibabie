The one that needs no work
The action green is surface-independent. #1ED760 with a #04140D label measures 9.85:1 on paper and 9.85:1 on charcoal — the ratio is between the button and its own label, so the page behind it is irrelevant. Picking a light-on-dark-agnostic action colour turns out to have bought the whole dark mode its anchor for free.
