Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to make an AI music video?

Most tracks render in 3–10 minutes end to end. Complex Studio projects with full storyboard review can take longer.

What audio can I bring?

Upload MP3, WAV, or M4A files up to 200MB (tracks up to 10 minutes). Video files work too — drop in an MP4 or MOV and Solmi pulls the audio out. You can also paste a Suno, YouTube, or TikTok link.

Do I need the rights to the song?

Yes. Only use music you own or have permission to use — your original songs and tracks you made with Suno or Udio are fine. You confirm ownership before every render.

What formats and resolutions can I export?

Finished videos export as MP4 in 16:9, 9:16, or 1:1. Free previews render at 480p; 720p and 1080p exports are included with paid plans and credit packs. Projects stay saved as editable timelines.

Is there a free plan?

Yes — you get 100 music video credits when you sign up, no credit card required. Paid plans and one-time credit packs unlock watermark-free exports and longer videos.

Does Solmi handle lyrics and other languages?

Lyrics are auto-detected and lip-synced with word-level timing, and you can burn styled captions into the export. Lyric detection works natively in 14 languages.
