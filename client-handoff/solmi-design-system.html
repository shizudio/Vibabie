One system, two dialects.

Everything below is the live CSS from the shipped solmi.ai, read off the site rather than redrawn. Copy any value, or take the whole token block at the foot of the page. The colour swatches, the buttons and the panels on this page are the components — inspect them.

Colour

Eight surface and text tokens, paired light → dark. Toggle the theme in the header and every swatch below repaints, because they are reading the variables rather than hard-coded values.

The action

One saturated colour in the entire interface. It is the same value in both themes because it is the brand, not a surface. Reserve it for the single action that costs the user time or credits — if two things on a screen are green, neither one is the action.

Typography

DM Sans carries everything you read. DM Mono carries everything you scan — labels, metadata, durations, specs, anything numeric. That split is the fastest way to tell a Solmi surface from a generic dark SaaS one, so keep it: never set metadata in the sans.

Display · 500 · −.03em Turn any song
Section · 21px · 500 Start from a setup
Body · 15px · 400 Bring a song and the agent drafts the treatment.

Tracking

Negative and proportional to size: the bigger the type, the tighter it sets. Display −.03em, headings −.02em, body −.011em, buttons −.018em. Mono labels go the other way — positive .10–.14em with uppercase, which is what makes them read as labels rather than as small text.

Components

Live. Hover the action button to see the sweep.

Action button

56 / 0 32 / r16 · 17px 700 at −.018em. Hover is the sweep, never a lighter green.

The hover state is a turquoise → green → ember gradient at 100°, 240% wide, drifting for 2.2s on a loop. The warm stops sit about 6% darker than their light-theme values so the ramp stays even on charcoal. It needs isolation:isolate on the button with the sweep at z-index:−1 — a plain absolute pseudo-element paints above the label and hides it.

Elevation

One token, three stacked passes rather than a single large blur — that is what keeps it from looking like a drop shadow from 2012. Used on the composer and on floating surfaces only; panels in a stack use a hairline instead.

--lift
3 passes · .26–.30 alpha

Two dialects

The same tokens build both the marketing site and the product pages. What separates them is not colour or type — it is ratio. Getting this wrong is what makes a tool page read as a second landing page.

 Marketing (solmi.ai)Product (studio, tools)

The record and the drifting gallery are signature marketing devices. They should never appear on a product page — on their own they separate the two at a glance.

Standing rules

Settled decisions. They override anything that contradicts them.

01

One accent. The green is the only saturated colour in the chrome. Style accents tint state; they are not a second palette.

02

Hover on the action is the sweep. Not a lighter green, not an opacity change. It is the one piece of brand motion in the interface.

03

Mono for anything you scan. Durations, ratios, resolutions, credits, specs, labels. Sans for anything you read.

04

Every control states its cost. If a setting changes render time or credits, the surface says so next to the button — not in a tooltip, not on a pricing page.

05

Text clears 4.5:1. The muted token is a solid value, not an alpha, so it does not drift when the surface underneath changes.

06

Video tiles take one of two shapes — 16:9 for landscape, 3:4 for portrait. Never a raw 9:16: it becomes a skyscraper in any grid and gets sliced by its own frame.

07

One easing curve. cubic-bezier(.22,.61,.36,1) for everything. Micro 160ms, standard 200ms, entrances 300–450ms.
