Overview

Perena is a DeFi yield protocol built on Solana. As a founding team member, I led a creative and design team of three to four while owning growth, brand, product design, and business development. The pivotal shift for me was growing from a pure creative into a business-led growth person — one who identified product gaps through data and turned them into features.

My core belief

In a market where every protocol competes on yield, the differentiator is experience. I built that case by combining brand, product, and design decisions, turning each marketing campaign into an opportunity to bring delight to our users.

Product

Perena Purple

Perena Purple — invitation-only VIP dashboard

Perena Purple is the VIP program I conceived and led end-to-end, from PRD through engineering delivery to marketing launch. It was born from a single metric observation:

20% of our depositors held 70% of our AUM. Serious users deserved a more serious experience.

Purple was built around four improvements:

  1. An exclusive communication channel with direct access to founders and fund researchers.
  2. Priority product and protocol updates.
  3. A personalised dashboard.
  4. Macro market signals paired with protocol opinion pieces.

The goal was transparency, trust, and tangible value for the people whose capital mattered most to Perena.

I personally managed relationships with over 20 depositors at the $100K+ tier through bespoke outreach that converted conversations into committed capital.

To scale the operation, I built a VIP status visualiser from our depositor database and automated the content pipeline using a Claude project that transformed each communication brief into six formats for distribution across channels. I defined the communication tone — a senior female fund manager in a one-on-one client relationship — and used an AI model to polish every message in that voice: onboarding, weekly updates, yield-change alerts, and event invitations.

The system proved its worth under pressure. During market downturns and Solana-related exploit events, we reached Purple users immediately — clarifying how their funds were or weren't affected before uncertainty turned into withdrawal decisions. Engagement built trust. Trust reduced churn.

The long-term vision for Purple is a private banking experience built inside a DeFi protocol.

Beyond Purple — how I operated

I operated as the translation layer between protocol mechanics and user experience. I attended product standups to catch UX implications early, crafted PRDs to communicate with engineers without overhead, and worked with designers on briefs we executed together — everyone with enough creative ownership and clear constraints to ship fast, considered output.

I facilitated the upgrade of the invest page, directing the product designer to transform a plain table of products into consistent cards that could hold investment thesis and product mechanics. I caught experience failures before they shipped: an invest page that needed filtering logic before it could scale; withdrawal copy that defaulted to USD* across all assets rather than adapting per-product. I also personally deployed several frontend features directly.

Brand

Building an identity from a logo and a flower

Given only a logo and a floral concept to start, I built Perena's complete brand identity from the ground up — visual system, tone of voice, mascot character world, merch line, and all customer-facing content. It has since become one of the most well-liked brand identities in the Solana ecosystem.

My intention was for people to remember Perena as the place where they feel safe, confident, and delighted to grow their wealth. The brand needed to feel emotional, personal, and categorically different from everything else that is hyper-marketed in the space.

Imagine growing your money the way you wait for a flower to bloom.

I built Perena's identity at the intersection of two worlds that don't usually meet: the cold, hard mechanics of finance and the warmth of organic nature. Where other protocols use charts and grids, Perena uses wildflower meadows, vault doors hidden in gardens, cherry blossoms surrounding gold bars. Flowers became the core motif — a visual metaphor for yield, patience, and growth.

In terms of colour, we wanted the brand to feel light, luxurious, and whimsical — realistic and cinematic to evoke trust, but also surreally beautiful, a glimpse into a wonderfully wealthy future. We almost romanticised finance: it no longer needs to feel cold or scary, but like a function that welcomes abundance and happiness.

Deep Violet is our primary colour: warm enough to feel emotional, authoritative enough to feel trustworthy, and deliberately distant from the cold blues of corporate fintech.

Gold is secondary: the quiet signal of value — a ring on a hand, a keyhole on a vault door, an embossed bar. It never dominates.

Gradient iridescence is our accent: glitter, shimmer, holographic light. It anchors Perena in the digital world without relying on generic web3 tropes.

The feeling I wanted to create: arriving somewhere beautiful. That your money is held somewhere considered, even lavish, but never ostentatious. A private garden with a locked door — and you have the key.
